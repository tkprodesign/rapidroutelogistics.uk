### Tracking System Upgrade (Admin Control Panel + Customer Tracking Page)

The shipment event system and tracking page should be upgraded together so they work as a complete professional logistics platform rather than as separate features.

#### 1. Redesign the Admin "Add Shipping Location Event" Form

The current form is heavily focused on road-based shipments and assumes every update has a city, state, and postal code. This does not reflect real-world international logistics where shipments may travel by sea, air, rail, or through ports, customs facilities, warehouses, and distribution hubs.

**Required Improvements:**

* Add **Transport Mode**:

  * Road
  * Air
  * Sea
  * Rail
  * Mixed

* Add **Event Type**:

  * Shipment Created
  * Picked Up
  * Departed Facility
  * Arrived Facility
  * Customs Clearance
  * Customs Hold
  * Port Arrival
  * Port Departure
  * Vessel Departure
  * Vessel Arrival
  * Warehouse Processing
  * In Transit
  * Out For Delivery
  * Delivered
  * Delayed
  * Exception
  * Payment Required
  * Other

* Add **Location Type**:

  * Warehouse
  * Distribution Center
  * Airport
  * Seaport
  * Customs Office
  * Checkpoint
  * Vessel
  * Aircraft
  * Customer Address
  * Other

* Keep:

  * Tracking Number
  * Status Text
  * Event Severity
  * Location Name
  * Country

* Make these optional:

  * City
  * State/Region
  * Postal Code

* Payment fields should only appear when a payment-related event is selected.

* For sea shipments, support optional fields such as:

  * Vessel Name
  * Voyage Number
  * Port of Departure
  * Port of Arrival

This will allow realistic shipment histories for sea freight, air freight, customs processing, warehousing, and local delivery.

---

#### 2. Upgrade the Customer Tracking Page

The tracking page should feel comparable to premium logistics companies and present information in a more useful way.

##### Status First

Instead of leading with the tracking number, prominently display:

* Current Status Badge
* Tracking Number
* Origin → Destination
* Last Updated Timestamp

Example:

🟢 In Transit

Tracking #: XXXXXXXX

New York, USA → London, UK

Last Updated: June 3, 2026 14:35 UTC

---
